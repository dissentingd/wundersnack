About wundersnack

The natural world is full of wonder most of us never get to see: a mushroom that glows, a single cell with a glass skeleton, a star orbiting a black hole. wundersnack is a quick break to meet some of it.

No accounts, no likes, no comments, no tracking. Just look for a minute and feel a bit better.

Helped? If you tap a thumb, we count one anonymous up or down for that item (nothing about you or your device is sent), so we can find and show more of what actually helps. What you've seen and how you voted is remembered only in this browser, so you keep meeting new things.

Every picture and video is shown with its creator's credit, license and a link to where it lives. (Videos from Wikimedia Commons are converted once into a format every phone can play.) Sources: iNaturalist observers, NASA, ESO, Wikimedia Commons, and hand-picked videos embedded from their original channels. Facts come from Wikipedia (CC BY-SA).
